COLLEGE CAMPUS FACILITIES & HOSTEL REGULATIONS

1. HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION & MESS SERVICES:
- Room Allotment: Hostel rooms (Double sharing and Triple sharing) are allocated based on merit and distance from hometown.
- Annual Hostel Fee:
  * Double Sharing with AC: INR 95,000 per year (including mess charges)
  * Non-AC Sharing: INR 70,000 per year (including mess charges)
  * Refundable caution deposit: INR 5,000 at time of admission.
- Mess Timings:
  * Breakfast: 07:30 AM – 09:00 AM
  * Lunch: 12:30 PM – 02:00 PM
  * Snacks: 04:45 PM – 05:45 PM
  * Dinner: 07:30 PM – 09:30 PM
- Curfew & In-Out Timings:
  * All hostel residents must return inside hostel premises before 09:30 PM.
  * Night outpass requires prior written approval from parent/guardian via the warden portal 24 hours in advance.

2. CENTRAL LIBRARY & DIGITAL RESOURCE CENTER:
- Working Hours:
  * Monday to Friday: 08:00 AM to 10:00 PM
  * Saturday & Sunday: 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM
  * During Exam Period: Open until 11:30 PM
- Book Borrowing Quota:
  * Undergraduate students: 4 books for 14 days (renewable once).
  * Late return overdue charge: INR 2 per book per day.
- Digital Library: Provides free access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, Springer, and ACM Digital Library via campus network.

3. CAMPUS HEALTH CLINIC & AMBULANCE:
- 24/7 on-campus medical health center with resident medical officer and nursing staff.
- Ambulance service is available round-the-clock for emergencies (Ext: 108 / +91-1234567890).
- Free basic medications and emergency first aid provided to all enrolled students and staff.

4. CAMPUS WI-FI ACCESS:
- High-speed 1 Gbps Wi-Fi is available across all academic blocks, hostels, and library.
- Registration: Connect to "College-Secure", login using Roll Number and College Portal Password. Up to 2 devices per student.
