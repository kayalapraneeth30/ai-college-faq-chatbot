COLLEGE EXAMINATION GUIDELINES & PROCEDURES

1. EXAMINATION SCHEME & WEIGHTAGE:
- Continuous Internal Assessment (CIE): 30% weightage.
  * Mid-Term Exam 1: 15 marks
  * Mid-Term Exam 2: 15 marks
  * Assignments, quizzes, and classroom attendance: 10 marks (normalized to 30)
- Semester End Examinations (SEE): 70% weightage.
  * Conducted over 3 hours duration for each theory course.
  * Passing minimum: 35% in SEE (25 out of 70 marks) and an aggregate of 40% (40 out of 100 marks) combined CIE + SEE.

2. HALL TICKET & ADMIT CARD:
- Digital Hall tickets are issued via the student portal (portal.college.edu) 7 days before examination commencement.
- Mandatory requirements to download Hall Ticket:
  * Minimum 75% attendance confirmed by respective department HOD.
  * No outstanding dues certificate from accounts and central library.
- Students must carry the printed Hall Ticket and physical Student ID card to each exam session. Entry without these credentials is strictly prohibited.

3. REVALUATION & CHALLENGE EVALUATION:
- Students dissatisfied with their evaluation can apply for revaluation within 15 calendar days from the date of result declaration.
- Revaluation fee: INR 500 per subject.
- Challenge valuation is permitted if the student remains dissatisfied after revaluation. Challenge evaluation fee is INR 5,000 per subject (refundable if the grade improves by more than 15%).

4. SUPPLEMENTARY / BACKLOG EXAMINATIONS:
- Supplementary exams for odd semesters are conducted alongside even semester exams, and vice versa.
- Students can register for a maximum of 4 backlog courses per supplementary session.
- Backlog registration fee: INR 350 per course.
