COLLEGE ADMISSIONS, SCHOLARSHIPS & FEE STRUCTURE

1. FEE SCHEDULE & PAYMENT DEADLINES:
- Tuition fees for Odd Semesters (Sem 1, 3, 5, 7): Must be paid on or before August 15th of each academic year.
- Tuition fees for Even Semesters (Sem 2, 4, 6, 8): Must be paid on or before January 15th of each academic year.
- Late Payment Penalty:
  * Up to 7 days late: INR 50 per day late fine.
  * 8 to 20 days late: Flat INR 1,500 penalty.
  * After 20 days: Student portal registration is blocked until approved by the Dean of Finance.

2. ACCEPTED PAYMENT MODES:
- Online payments through the student ERP portal via Net Banking, UPI, Credit/Debit cards.
- Demand Draft (DD) in favor of "Principal, Engineering College" payable at campus bank branch.
- Cash payments are strictly not accepted at the cash counter.

3. SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
- Merit Scholarship: Top 5% students of each branch with CGPA > 9.0 receive a 25% tuition fee waiver for the subsequent academic year.
- Sports & Cultural Scholarship: 15% to 50% tuition fee waiver for students representing the university at state or national levels.
- Government Scholarships: Eligible SC/ST/OBC/EWS students can apply for State Post-Matric Scholarships and National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes. The college scholarship cell assists with document verification.

4. REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY:
- Cancellation requested before orientation: 100% refund minus INR 1,000 processing charges.
- Cancellation requested within 15 days of semester commencement: 80% tuition fee refund.
- No refund is applicable after 30 days of semester commencement.
